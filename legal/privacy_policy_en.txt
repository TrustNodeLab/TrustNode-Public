2. WHAT DATA WE PROCESS
- Voice data (biometric personal data) — only with explicit separate consent under Art. 11 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ, processed solely on the user's device
- Message text for fraud analysis — processed exclusively locally, not shared with third parties
- Screen screenshots — analyzed locally, personally identifiable information (PII) is automatically masked before processing
- Behavioral data (centroids) — anonymous statistical models of user behavior used to improve fraud detection accuracy. Contains no personal data, stored in encrypted form

3. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING
Protection of the user from telephone fraud and social engineering (Art. 6 para. 1 clause 5 of 152-FZ — protection of life and health)

4. LEGAL BASIS
Consent of the personal data subject (Art. 6 para. 1 clause 1 of 152-FZ) and protection of legitimate rights and interests (Art. 6 para. 1 clause 5 of 152-FZ)

5. DISCLOSURE TO THIRD PARTIES
All computations are performed on the user's device. Data is NOT transferred to external servers.

5.1. CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFER (Art. 12 of 152-FZ)
Not carried out.

6. DATA STORAGE AND DESTRUCTION
6.1. All data is processed in the device's RAM. Analysis results (threat metadata) are stored in an encrypted local database for no more than 24 hours, after which they are automatically deleted.
6.2. Call recordings (.tnc audio files) are stored temporarily in encrypted form (AES-256-GCM) for no more than 24 hours. Automatic deletion occurs at each app launch and via the periodic cleanup mechanism (WorkManager). Recordings are deleted after speech recognition completes or after 24 hours — whichever comes first.
6.3. Unencrypted analytical data (screenshots, message text) is NOT saved to disk.
6.4. Behavioral data (centroids) is stored in encrypted form and deleted when all app data is removed.
6.5. Personal data is destroyed by deleting the relevant records from the encrypted database and clearing SharedPreferences upon expiry of the retention period, upon consent revocation, or upon the data subject's request.
6.6. Users may delete all app data through device settings (App Data → Clear) or through the app interface (Settings → Privacy → Delete My Data).
